How Dorothy AI works

Retrieval first. Honesty always.

1 · Safety first

If a message suggests someone may be at risk, crisis support appears before anything else.

2 · Retrieve

Your question is matched against a knowledge base written from official sources — in Tamil and English.

3 · Answer with sources

The answer shows its sources and the date a person last reviewed it. Nothing is invented.

4 · Hand to a human

When it doesn't know, it says so and connects you to the Dorothy team or a professional.

Dorothy AI gives information, not medical, legal or psychological advice. It runs in your browser: your questions are not sent anywhere or stored.
