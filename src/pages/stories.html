Milestones from Tamil Nadu
2017

K. Prithika Yashini

After a legal fight that reached the Madras High Court, she took up duty as a police sub-inspector in Tamil Nadu in 2017. She is recognised as India's first transgender woman sub-inspector.

2019

Narthaki Nataraj

A Bharatanatyam artist from Madurai, she became the first transgender woman to receive the Padma Shri in 2019, an honour she says was earned on merit, as an artist.

Read more ↗
2013

Grace Banu

Widely reported as the first transgender person admitted to an engineering college in Tamil Nadu, she went on to found the Trans Rights Now Collective and campaign for reservations.
